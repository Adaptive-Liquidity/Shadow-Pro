## Negative findings / unresolved issues

- Official sources retrieved here do **not** substantiate an independent “Astralane + Lil’ JIT” multi-relay requirement. The final plan will treat all non-Jito relay integrations as blocked until their official API/atomicity/privacy/idempotency documentation is pinned.
- The bundle topology must not put a DontFront-marked TX-1 after an optional provision transaction within the same Jito bundle. Provisioning must occur before opportunity detection or in a separate bundle; otherwise the bundle is rejected under the Jito ordering rule.
- The earlier wording that a tip is “only paid if bundle lands” must be stated conditionally: if it is an instruction in a bundle that does not land, it does not execute. The final plan will not generalize this to all submission methods without source-backed qualification.

## Remote-signing design correction

The SignRequest’s `transaction_message_hash` is an integrity-binding value for the authorization envelope only. The signing provider must receive the **exact serialized Solana message bytes** or an algorithm-equivalent source-backed format that produces the protocol-required Ed25519 signature. A provider may never mutate blockhash, compute budget, fee payer, instructions, or signatures after deterministic manifest validation without forcing a new manifest, simulation, policy decision, and signature request.

## Architecture refinement

The canonical topology will describe **logical settlement stages** inside TX-1 rather than claim unverified Shadow Paymaster entry instructions can surround an external flash loan/swap sequence. The actual code-level instruction ordering and whether settlement occurs through dedicated on-chain instructions or verified off-chain balance proofs are marked for pinned IDL and transaction-size/CU feasibility testing. The invariant is unchanged: borrow, route, and repay occur together in the one transaction; payout transfers remain after successful flash-loan execution, and all serialised bytes must be pre-approved.
